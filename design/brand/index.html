~/brand

Penumbra on Axiom.
One low Sun on a black terminal.

The brand system for Penumbra: Axiom's flat dark surfaces and monospace, with one orange, ember, for the Sun and the wordmark kept from Low Sun. Source of truth: DESIGN.md and tokens.css.

~/logo

Wordmark

Kept unchanged from Low Sun: Archivo italic 800, wdth 112, letters in paper, the half-risen Sun in ember on a hairline horizon. It is the only text not set in mono.

Sizes and small mark

Clear space: the height of the letter P on every side. Dark grounds only (void, carbon, graphite). Don't recolour the Sun, don't stretch, don't set the wordmark in mono.

~/rules

Do and don't

The short version of DESIGN.md sections 5 and 6.

Do

  • Set every line in IBM Plex Mono, except the wordmark.
  • Layer void → carbon → graphite → iron for depth.
  • Use 2px corners on cards, panels and buttons.
  • Open each section with a ~/section prompt.
  • Follow action labels with →.
  • Put a FAKE pill on any number that is not real.

Don't

  • Gradients, glows, or shadows for elevation.
  • Ember on body text, or on any chart line except the Sun.
  • NASA blue as a thin line or text on black.
  • Red or green for status. Use ✓ and ✕.
  • Light-theme sections or photography.
  • Corners above 2px, except pills.
