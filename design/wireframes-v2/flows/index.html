Wireframes v2 · flows (reference): three whole-app directions

Round 2, built from docs/IDEA.md, SCIENCE-SPEC.md and ARCHITECTURE.md. Each direction is the whole app, not one screen. Every chart and headline number on these pages comes from one shared FAKE model, so a picture can never disagree with its number. Greyscale only: the Low Sun brand is applied in the hi-fi pass. Round 1 is still in ../wireframes/.

A · Planner console

  • For: CLPS/Artemis planners doing real work.
  • Shape: one linked screen. Map → panorama → timelines → headline, left to right, in the order the engine computes them.
  • Strong: everything visible at once; every input shows what it reruns.
  • Weak: dense; judges need the story told for them.
Open A →

B · Story first

  • For: judges and the public, then planners.
  • Shape: six chapters that follow the 2-minute demo: IM-2 → same site → move it → rank → proof → use it. The headline number follows you in a side rail.
  • Strong: the demo video is the page; the IM-2 pit model gets the spotlight.
  • Weak: slower for repeat use; needs "Skip to planner".
Open B →

C · Compare and rank

  • For: "where and when should we land?"
  • Shape: region heatmap and a region × date matrix on top; two site-date pairs (A vs B) below, with the difference spelled out.
  • Strong: shows choice, not just one answer; uses the Should-tier heatmap well.
  • Weak: depends on baked grids (Should tier); thin if those slip.
Open C →

All three share the same edge cases: states.html (loading, OUT_OF_COVERAGE, survives the span, NOT_LOADED, slip fallbacks, phone width).
