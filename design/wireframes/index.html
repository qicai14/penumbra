Penumbra lo-fi wireframes

5 screens × 3 structural directions. Greyscale on purpose: judge layout and flow, not colour. All numbers are FAKE. Pick one letter per screen (mixing parts is fine) and note it in design/DECISIONS.md.

1 · Horizon panorama + timelines Must

A Stacked stripOpen ↗

Wide 360° strip on top, timelines under it on one shared time cursor, headline numbers on the right.

+ Most familiar, easy to build. − Strip is flat, so tall terrain at low sun is cramped.

B Polar sky dialOpen ↗

Round fisheye sky plot with terrain mask at the rim, plus a table of events with ± times.

+ Striking and shows the full Sun loop. − Low-elevation detail is squeezed at the rim; needs a non-linear scale.

C Time-firstOpen ↗

A big Sun-elevation vs mask chart over 30 days; the horizon is a small snapshot that follows the cursor.

+ Answers "when" directly. − Less of a visual wow for the demo.

2 · Pin and date controls + survival headline Must

A Headline heroOpen ↗

The answer (operable days, lethal shadow, margin) is a big banner; controls in a left rail, map with a 1 km ring.

+ The hook is the first thing seen. − Less map space.

B Map consoleOpen ↗

Full-bleed map, inputs panel on the right, results in a sticky bottom bar like a GIS tool.

+ Feels like a pro tool, max map. − Headline is smaller.

C What-if compareOpen ↗

Baseline plus variant cards (pin +300 m, date +2 d) side by side with deltas.

+ Matches demo step 3 exactly. − Needs several model runs per view.

3 · 3D terrain Should

A Full-bleed HUDOpen ↗

Edge-to-edge 3D with floating readout, layers and a time sweep bar.

+ Best for the video. − Overlays cover terrain.

B Linked splitOpen ↗

3D on the left, linked panorama, timeline and readout on the right, one cursor.

+ Ties the 3D to the numbers. − Busy on small screens.

C 2D map firstOpen ↗

Top-down map coloured by the model (shadow or operable days), 3D as an optional inset.

+ Survives cut-order 1 and doubles as the heatmap. − Less dramatic.

4 · IM-2 replay Should

A Scroll storyOpen ↗

Five scroll steps that follow the demo story, with a sticky visual that changes per step.

+ Best for the public and the video. − Linear; planners may skip it.

B Mission scrubberOpen ↗

Event timeline on top; "what happened" next to "what Penumbra computes"; pit-depth slider.

+ Honest record vs model. − Needs sourced events (EVIDENCE.md).

C As landed vs betterOpen ↗

Two columns: IM-2 as landed and the nearest site within 1 km that would have lasted 5+ days.

+ One-glance payoff for judges. − Depends on the site search working.

5 · Validation scoreboard Must (V1, V5)

A Ledger tableOpen ↗

One row per test: judge, pass rule, our value, spread, status. Rows expand.

+ Dense and scientific. − Plain for the video.

B Gauge cardsOpen ↗

Big "3/5" summary and a card per test with a value-vs-threshold gauge.

+ Readable at a glance on a slide. − Hides method detail.

C List and detailOpen ↗

Test list on the left; the picked test's charts (scatter, error histogram) and method on the right.

+ Shows the real evidence. − More charts to build.
