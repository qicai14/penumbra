## Ambition pass: "rank landing regions" (T24 + T25)

- **Goal beyond the goal:** a planner looking at one site learns whether a nearby spot or date would have kept the lander alive, and by how much. Today the app answers one point at a time.
- **Types:** Altitude (it serves a decision) and Depth (the grid has to be as trustworthy as the live engine). Range is light: a cartographer's colour and contour habits only.
- **Altitude ladder:**
  1. A heatmap of operable days over one region (T24 as written).
  2. **Get to the decision:** from the pinned site, the nearest grid point within 1 km that gives more operable days, plus the nearest landing date that does (IDEA §"Primary": "replay IM-2's site and date, then find the nearest site within 1 km, or the nearest landing date, that would have given it more than 5 days"). On Mons Mouton this becomes the demo sentence: "IM-2 would have had N days 400 m east".
  3. Rank all nine regions. Blocked: no cited centres (#26). Step down to rung 2.
- **Depth:**
  1. A top illumination scientist would show where the map is *uncertain*, not only where it is bright. Grid cells whose operable days change inside the σ_z band get hatched.
  2. Start from the shipped tiles and the same `core` functions the browser runs, never a re-implementation.
  3. Method: a native grid run on 8 threads, storing u16 operable hours and first-lethal time per cell per date (about 110 KB per date), with a sequential colour scale and one contour at the preset's "≥ 5 days" line.
  4. Outside judge: the live WASM engine. Five random cells per date must equal the in-browser result at that site and date (same 600 s step, exact). The illumination share of the grid's cells must also agree with AVGVISIB at the V2 tolerance, which re-uses an existing judge.
- **Hard target:** the whole Mons Mouton usable area (16.7 km, about 28 000 cells at 100 m) in under 1 min on 8 threads (10 ms × 28 000 / 8 ≈ 35 s).
- **Needs:** tiles, engine and ephemeris are there. Region centres for the other seven are **missing** (rung 3 only). IM-2-date cells must fall inside a shipped ephemeris segment for the live check.
